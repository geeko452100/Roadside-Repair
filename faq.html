Getting started

How do I get an estimate?

Call us or send the form on our Get in Touch page. We'll schedule a free site visit, usually within a week, and follow up with an itemized, fixed-price proposal in about two weeks.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Great Bend and regularly work across Barton, Ellis, Ellsworth, Pawnee, Rice, Russell, Rush, and Stafford counties. For larger commercial jobs we'll travel farther, so just ask.

Do you work from my plans or provide design?

Both. We can build from plans your architect has drawn, or take you through design-build with our in-house drafter and partner engineers.

How far out are you booking?

Most remodels start 4–8 weeks after contract signing. New homes and commercial projects are scheduled around permitting, typically 2–3 months out.

Pricing & payments

Are your bids fixed-price?

Yes. Our proposals are itemized and fixed-price. The only things that change the price are changes you request or hidden conditions we couldn't reasonably see, and those always come to you as a written change order before any work happens.

What does the payment schedule look like?

Typically a 10% deposit at signing, then progress payments tied to completed milestones (foundation, framing, dry-in, and so on). You never pay ahead of the work.

Do you help with financing?

We don't lend directly, but we work with several local banks on construction-to-permanent loans and can provide the documentation they need.

During construction

Who do I talk to while the job is underway?

You'll have one project manager as your point of contact, plus a superintendent on site every working day. You'll get photo updates every week.

Do you pull permits and schedule inspections?

Always. Permits, inspections, and utility locates are part of our scope and included in your price.

Can we live in the house during a remodel?

Usually, yes. We set up dust barriers, protect floors, and clean up every afternoon. For kitchen remodels we can help you plan a temporary kitchen.

What happens if weather causes delays?

Kansas weather is part of the plan. Our schedules include weather days, and we'll tell you right away if a storm pushes a milestone.

After the build

What warranty do you offer?

A 10-year workmanship warranty on new construction and 2 years on remodels, in addition to manufacturer warranties on materials and equipment.

Do you come back for small fixes?

Yes. We do an 11-month check-in on every new home to take care of settling cracks, sticky doors, and the like at no charge.

Got a project in mind?

Tell us what you're planning. We'll walk the site and send a written estimate, free.
