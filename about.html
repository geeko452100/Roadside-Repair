Our story

It started with a pickup and a nail gun.

Dale Acheson founded Acme in 1998 as a two-person framing crew working for other builders. Word got around that his walls were plumb and his crews showed up when they said they would. Within a few years, homeowners were calling Dale directly.

Today Acme is a full-service general contractor with 38 full-time tradespeople, our own concrete and framing crews, and a shop full of equipment. Dale still walks every site on Fridays.
